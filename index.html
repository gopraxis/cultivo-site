The problem

The plan lives in someone's head.

Most grows plan in a spreadsheet, on a whiteboard, or in the head grower's memory. And planning is the easy part. The damage happens when the schedule moves.

FIG. 01

A room flips a week late

One IPM hold, one slow strain, one crew short-week. Small on its own. Never small downstream.

FIG. 02

Nobody downstream hears

The nursery keeps cutting to the old date. Post-production staffs for a harvest that isn't coming. The gap compounds in silence.

FIG. 03

Nobody can reconstruct why

Three months later the numbers are short and the answer is a shrug. The record of what changed, when, and why is the most valuable data your facility produces. Almost nobody keeps it.

What Cultivo is

One system. Three layers.

01

The record

Cultivation, post-production, inventory, and orders on one operational ledger, from clone to invoice. We fit it to your facility during implementation, not the other way around: your rooms, your strains, your grades, your SOPs. It becomes the system of record for how material actually moves, and the crew stops working around the software.

02

The planner

Plan cycles against real room capacity, months out. When a date moves, Cultivo shows the downstream ripple before you commit it, keeps plan against actual, and writes every change to a ledger anyone can read later. "What happened in March" becomes a thirty-second answer instead of an argument.

03

The intelligence layer The subscription

An operator agent with your whole ledger in its head. It lives where your team already works and answers anything about the operation. More importantly, it does not wait to be asked: it briefs you weekly, flags variance and loss as they form, and hands your investors a clean picture monthly. It compounds on your own data, which means it gets sharper every month you run on it.

Why it's different

Seed-to-sale software has meant the same thing for a decade: a compliance checklist with screens, built to satisfy a regulator and fought by every person who touches it. Cultivo was built the other way around.

It started inside a working facility and got shaped by daily production until it fit. Compliance falls out of a truthful ledger. It is not the product. The way your facility runs is the product.
