The agent

Keep the plan current without chasing updates

When something drifts, Cultivo asks the accountable person one plain question. After confirmation, it updates the affected dates, records who approved the change, and tells downstream teams what moved.

  • It asks before it writes. A person confirms every proposed change. Cultivo records the approval and never edits the operation silently.
  • It speaks the crew's language. Harvest pushed two days? Clones cut this morning? Plants moved to veg? Answer in a sentence; Cultivo handles the update.
  • It surfaces conflicts without stopping work. The crew keeps moving while Cultivo sends the right issue to the right person.
  • It works where the crew already talks. Use Slack or WhatsApp without rolling out another floor application.
