The agent

The hired hand that never sleeps

Most systems rot because updating them is someone's second job. Cultivo's agent makes it nobody's job. It watches the plan against the floor, notices what drifted, and asks the one accountable person one plain question.

  • It asks before it writes. Every change is proposed, confirmed by a human, applied through a governed path, and receipted. No silent edits, ever.
  • It speaks grower. Harvest pushed two days? Clones cut this morning? Plants moved to veg? Answer in a sentence; the ledger does the typing.
  • It escalates, it never blocks. The physical work never waits on software. Conflicts become a morning brief, not a locked screen.
  • Slack at home, WhatsApp abroad. The channel is a setting, not a rollout.
